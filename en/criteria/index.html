How we rate

These ratings exist so you can choose a night by sound and by venue character. Editors assess venues on six criteria on the ground, and select S/A nights from every Tokyo club event gathered nightly. Listing fees never influence a rating.

Three labels

Music-first venues put sound first; party venues put the crowd first. Mixed venues can be either, depending on the night.

  • Music-first The sound is the point. People come for the booking and the system, and they stay on the floor.
  • Party The crowd is the point. Tables and meeting people come first; who is playing matters less.
  • Mixed Depends on the night. The crowd changes with the weekday or the event.

This is not a ranking. Music-first is not “better” than Party; it tells you what people go there for.

Six criteria

Before you go: booking, sound, floor vs tables, crowd, pricing, dress code. Each is checked on the ground.

  • Booking — who plays. How often international guests come, the domestic line-ups, the residents.
  • Sound — the system and how it is tuned. Low end, how the booth is handled, how the floor hears it.
  • Floor vs tables — how the space is split. How much room is actually kept for dancing.
  • Crowd — who shows up. Age, intent, share of visitors from abroad, how a first-timer is treated.
  • Pricing — door and drink levels, discounts, how guest lists work.
  • Dress code — what is enforced, and how closely the door looks.

Each venue page carries a short note per criterion. Where we have not written one yet it shows “—”; we do not pad it.

Tiers S and A

S/A is a selection of nights by visiting headliners and crowd scale. It is not a score of your taste.

  • S — a standout night, chosen by visiting headliners and crowd scale. ★ marks nights where that visiting artist is the main draw.
  • A — a night worth shortlisting, chosen on the same basis: an international guest in a small room, a ticketed night drawing a crowd, a small room with notable turnout.
  • B — not on the front page. Shown only in a venue’s “Nights here, next 30 days”.

We work from Resident Advisor listings, the venues’ own schedules, and word of mouth we collect in Japan. In scope: dance music, hip-hop and all-mix nights. Anime/idol events and live concerts are not listed.

Listing fees and ratings

Listing fees never influence a rating. Paying does not change an assessment.

If we ever run ads or paid slots, they never touch the labels, the six criteria or the tiers. Any paid slot will be marked as such. Invitations or guest-list spots from venues and promoters do not change a rating.

Updates

Event data is refreshed automatically every night. Venue ratings are revised as we learn more; each page shows its last update and a confidence level (high, medium, low) — how sure we are about that venue.

Corrections and objections

Corrections and objections get a reply within seven days. Counter-statements are published alongside the rating so you can read both.

Venue owners and staff can send corrections or objections by email. A counter-statement is published on that venue’s page, next to our rating, as written. Factual errors are checked and fixed, and the fix date becomes the page’s update date.
