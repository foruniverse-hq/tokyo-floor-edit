What to expect here

A long-running club and live house that opened in 1996, with a capacity of 600 according to RA. Its official October 2026 schedule mixes hip-hop live shows, dancehall, house and rock; the Thursday series "clubasia THURSDAY" is techno/house (Juan Atkins played in September 2026), and eplus lists mainly band and hip-hop live shows. Sound-focused nights and live or party nights coexist, so it is rated mixed, and there are no signs of pickup-oriented features such as ladies-free nights.
