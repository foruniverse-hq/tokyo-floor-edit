What to expect here

Opened in 2013 (formerly ROCKWEST), it is a rental venue where promoters bring in their own events, and its official site lists floor rental from ¥30,000 per hour. Its October 2026 schedule has RA-listed techno, house and psytrance (UNCHARTED TIME, SOLIDDRUM, R-TRANCE) on 6F at night, but 7F and the daytime slots are mostly hip-hop, UMB qualifiers, Vocaloid/anime-club nights and all-mix, so the character splits and it is rated mixed. There is no evidence of ladies-free nights or street touts; the size is estimated from floor area and is borderline small.
