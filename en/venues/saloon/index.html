What to expect here

A sister venue of Daikanyama UNIT, this DJ bar and club is on the third basement floor. It restarted at the end of 2022 and is also used for all-night events that run across all UNIT floors. The official site lists events for October and November 2026 (KALT, DROPZ 10th and others); the capacity of 100 comes from technoairlines.
