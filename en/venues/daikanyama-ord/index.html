What to expect here

Opened in May 2025 as a sister venue of Batica, solfa and COUNTER CLUB, with d&b sound systems on both floors. It officially calls itself "all-genre": Wednesdays are four-on-the-floor nights and Thursdays a hip-hop lounge, and RA lists two-floor events pairing techno and tech house with afrobeat and hip-hop, so its character changes nightly and it is rated mixed. There are no signs of street touts or ladies-free nights, and it leans toward the sound-focused side.
