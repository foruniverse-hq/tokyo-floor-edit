About this venue

Opening May 2025, Daikanyama ORD. is a sister venue to Batica/solfa/COUNTER CLUB, featuring d&b sound systems on both floors. With an official 'all-genre' stance, it shifts nightly—Wednesday's four-on-the-floor, Thursday's hip-hop lounge, and RA's techno/tech house plus afrobeat/hip-hop on two floors, blending without typical 'chill' or 'ladies' free' elements.
