What to expect here

A church-themed bar and event space run by LD&K. RA lists LD&K-hosted techno, house and D&B nights through July and August 2026, with entry from free to ¥1,000. Time Out presents it as a bar to stop at before heading to the next club, so it leans toward a concept bar and could be considered out of scope; with no evidence of EDM/Top 40 or a pickup scene it is placed among sound-focused venues, but with low confidence.
