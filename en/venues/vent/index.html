What to expect here

VENT has a capacity of 400 on RA and bills itself as "selected house and techno". It is a sound-focused two-room club with a TAGUCHI sound system and sound-absorbing panels, and international guests play every weekend through October to December 2026 (Petar Dundov, Claudio PRC, Lee Burridge, Marcellus Pittman, ANNĒ; over 100 events a year on RA). Guides in Japan and abroad describe the crowd as mostly there for the music, with no mention of a pickup scene.
