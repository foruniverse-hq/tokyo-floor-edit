What to expect here

A 100-capacity DJ bar that opened in July 2019 with Kenji Takimi and Force Of Nature (RA News). It is audio-minded, with Pioneer speakers, a rotary mixer and Lowther speakers in the VIP room, and a renovation in summer 2025 doubled the speakers and enlarged the floor (Mixmag Asia). Music-led bookings are listed for October to December 2026, so it is still open.
