What to expect here

Roppongi Cube (RA venue #10748) is a small basement event space that takes DJ events, live shows, dance battles and private hire. RA lists underground events such as GABBER'S NOT DEAD and HALI, but Tokyo Club Map files it as a "club/disco" for hip-hop, house, all-mix and pops, so its character depends on the event. It has RA listings for 2025 and 2026.
