What to expect here

A large hip-hop-only club that opened in 1997; deeptokyo lists a capacity of up to 1,000 and Mapple says about 1,000 people come each time, with queues of general customers on weekends. It has a history of cheaper entry for women (2017 RA: Ladies ¥2,000 / Men ¥3,000), and as a hip-hop-focused venue with a mainstream crowd it is classed as a pickup-oriented party club, but it is also a mecca for hip-hop fans with MC battles and dance showcases, so it is not purely a pickup spot. e+ lists shows for October and November 2026, confirming it is still open.
