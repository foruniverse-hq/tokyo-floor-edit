What to expect here

Opened in August 2024 on the site of Aoyama ZERO, which closed in June 2024. It inherited ZERO's analog sound system as is (Time Out calls it the best sound system in Tokyo), and the owner is a former resident DJ. RA keeps listing it in 2026 (the monthly "Prisma" on the second Sunday, "Minimal" in May, "Parallel" in July and more, house-leaning at around ¥1,000 entry), making it a typical music-led small club; capacity is not published.
