What to expect here

Opened in 2022 as a house-focused club and relaunched in 2024 as a bar-lounge and club in the "LUXURY LOUNGE BRAND" group. The official October 2026 schedule has TECHNO/HOUSE with a free MUSIC BAR on weekdays and ALL MIX or HIPHOP/R&B on weekends, so the character changes by night, and RA also lists house and techno events. Entry is ¥2,000 for both men and women, with no ladies-free offer.
