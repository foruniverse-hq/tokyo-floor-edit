What to expect here

Time Out describes it as "a small Nakameguro club known to fans of underground music", with a dance floor for about 70 people. RA keeps listing its events, including anniversary parties since it opened in 2008 and a New Year's Party on January 9, 2026 with tofubeats and XTAL. No closure has been reported, but operation after January 2026 is not confirmed.
