What to expect here

A DJ bar that opened in March 2019, at the same time as the O-EAST renovation. Its concept is sound-absorbing panels and sake cocktails, techno and house DJs play almost every night, and when it joins forces with O-EAST for "MIDNIGHT EAST" (for example Dixon in March 2026) it works as the second floor. RA lists events through October 2026, so it is still open; on its own it is a small, sound-focused bar, but during MIDNIGHT EAST it is effectively part of a large venue.
