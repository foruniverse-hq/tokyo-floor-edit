About this venue

Aoyama Hachi, opened in 1995, celebrates its 30th anniversary in November 2025 with four days of five parties featuring over 200 acts. The 2nd floor is a DJ bar, 3rd floor main dance floor, and 4th floor lounge, known for its 'Morning Bee' (from opening until noon). It won Time Out Tokyo's 2022 Love Local Awards for Best Club, with a full schedule for October 2026 on its website. Hip-hop plays, but the crowd is music-loving locals in the underground scene, with no pickup culture.
