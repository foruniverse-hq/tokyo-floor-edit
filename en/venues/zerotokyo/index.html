What to expect here

ZEROTOKYO has a capacity of 1,500 on RA (the space is shared with Zepp Shinjuku, and the tower's official floor guide also gives 1,500 standing), and its five areas across B1 to B4 make it one of Japan's largest clubs. From October to December 2026 it mixes mainstream nights (Axwell / Steve Angello, trance, an all-mix Halloween party, BRESH) with techno and house nights (SOUNDGATE with Takkyu Ishino, ESSENCE with Anfisa Letyago, Mau P, PRISM by All Underground). The crowd changes from night to night, so it is rated mixed.
