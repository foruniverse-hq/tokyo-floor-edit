About this venue

ZEROTOKYO, with a 1,500-capacity RA (sharing space with Zepp Shinjuku), features five zones across B1-B4, Japan's largest. From October to December 2026, it hosts diverse lineups including Axwell/Steve Angello, mainstream acts for Halloween, and techno/house DJs like SOUNDGATE, ESSENCE, Mau P, and PRISM, with shifting crowds nightly.
