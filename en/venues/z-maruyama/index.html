What to expect here

A small club "for dancing" opened on December 5, 2025 by the WOMB team. It has a four-point ADAMSON S10 system (three S10 units at each of four corners plus a subwoofer) tuned by Sonic Lab Audio, along with CDJ-3000s, and has been featured in the Mondo-dr Awards. Bookings are centered on house and techno, mixing veterans and younger DJs.
