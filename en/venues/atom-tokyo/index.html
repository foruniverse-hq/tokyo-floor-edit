What to expect here

Club atom opened in 2004 and was relaunched as ATOM TOKYO in 2015 as a three-floor club; Clubberia describes 4F as hip-hop/R&B, 5F as the all-mix main floor and 6F as a house/EDM lounge. After midnight entry starts at ¥3,000 for men and ¥1,500 for women, and guides explicitly call it a "pickup club" suitable for beginners, making it a leading Shibuya party club. Capacity is about 700, but as a three-floor flagship it is classed as large, and Shazam lists shows on October 31 and December 9, 2026, so it is judged to be still open.
