What to expect here

On the 7th floor of the Shinjuku Kaleido Building; the official site calls it one of the largest nightclubs in Japan, and private-hire information gives a capacity of 1,000. RA lists an "OPEN FORMAT PARTY" tagged Hip-Hop, open every day with free-entry campaigns and VIP table reservations, so its location, format and operation are all typical of a large mainstream club. The official site shows the October 2026 lineup, so it is open.
