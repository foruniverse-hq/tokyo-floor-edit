What to expect here

A hideaway event space in Daikanyama created in 2018 by the crew behind the outdoor festival Zipang. It has an RA venue page (#156224) and keeps running small, music-focused parties such as the monthly techno/house night "neutral" and a Neophoca minimal showcase, with listings confirmed for April to July 2026. There are no street touts or pickup-oriented features.
