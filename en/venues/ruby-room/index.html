What to expect here

A DJ bar and live space operating since 2002, with an open mic on Tuesdays, live shows plus DJs on Thursdays and DJ events on weekends; it has an RA venue page and 2025 listings. It has a JBL sound system but is not a serious sound-system venue, and it has no pickup-oriented features, so it is classed as a sound-focused small venue largely by elimination. As a live-bar hybrid it could be considered out of scope; the official site shows 2026 dates, so it is active.
