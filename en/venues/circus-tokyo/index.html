About this venue

CIRCUS Tokyo, featured in RA with over 140 annual bookings, celebrates its 11th anniversary in October–December 2026 with underground acts like Ben Sims, Makoto, Kasra, TSHA, and Silva Bumpa. Rated one of Tokyo's most popular underground clubs by Time Out, it maintains a private capacity (not listed on its official rental page, technoairlines), with a two-floor layout that avoids the typical 'small box' label.
