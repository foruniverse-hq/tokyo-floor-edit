About this venue

Opened in 2012, Aisotope Lounge is touted as Japan's largest LGBTQ+ club. Offering events like gay/lesbian parties, drag shows, and fan-focused gatherings with a focus on socializing over music, it's categorized as a 'chara-bako' (chatting club) rather than a music-first venue, differing from Shibuya/Roppongi-style pickup spots in clientele and culture. Current operations confirmed via e+ ticket sales for October 18, 2026, with a capacity of 250 (under 300, classified as a small venue).
