What to expect here

A small DJ bar that was a hub of Shibuya's underground scene from 2009. It closed its Shibuya location at the end of August 2025 because of rising rent and reopened in November 2025 in Kita-Magome, Ota Ward. It is now bistro-style and also open in the daytime, but DJ events continue and are still listed on RA; note that it is no longer a Shibuya venue.
