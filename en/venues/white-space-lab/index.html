What to expect here

A small, experimental gallery-style space in Maruyamacho. RA keeps listing techno events such as Polynasia (Deep Underground Techno Party) on the third Thursday of the month and "Disco Year One" (September 2026), and it is a regular spot for underground DJs, with advance tickets around ¥1,500 and ¥2,000 at the door. No public information is available on capacity or sound system.
