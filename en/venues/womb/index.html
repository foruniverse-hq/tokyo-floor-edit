What to expect here

WOMB's RA page lists a capacity of 1,000 across four floors, and through October and November 2026 it books international guests almost every night, mostly techno and house (Peter Van Hoesen, Bart Skils, DJ Nobu, Craig Richards, Hernan Cattaneo and others; over 200 events a year on RA). Domestic reviews say the crowd has few pickup-seekers and mostly comes for the music, and it is a regular in DJ Mag's Top 100. Its sister club Z Maruyama (capacity 200) opened in December 2025.
