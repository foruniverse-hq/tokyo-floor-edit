WOMB, a 1000-capacity venue with four floors, features top techno and house DJs like Peter Van Hoesen, Bart Skils, and Hernan Cattaneo, with over 200 international acts annually. Known for its music-focused crowd and frequent DJ Mag Top 100 appearances, it opens a sister venue, Z Maruyama, in December 2025.
