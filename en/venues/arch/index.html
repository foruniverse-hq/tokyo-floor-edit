What to expect here

There is no active "Arch" club in Shibuya; the one that fits is ArcH, an LGBTQ club in Shinjuku Nichome (closed in 2014 and reopened in 2025 in the Futami Building). Its highlights are MEN ONLY / WOMEN ONLY / MIX theme parties and drag shows, with music that changes nightly from anime songs to underground techno (for example GROOVE DISTORTION), so it is rated mixed. The official site lists events through the end of October 2026; it fits neither the sound-focused nor the pickup-oriented category, and a different Arch may have been meant.
