What to expect here

A tiny club in Hatagaya that opened around 2010, introduced by Time Out as a hangout for music lovers where you can play on the same level as the DJ. RA keeps listing experimental and bass-leaning events (DJ Sprinkles, Nick Klein, K/A/T/O MASSACRE and others, through December 2026), and the official site shows a daily schedule for October 2026, so it is open. It also hosts live shows and exhibitions, and is a purely underground, music-first venue.
