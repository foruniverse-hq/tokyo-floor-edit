What to expect here

A DJ bar in a detached house near Kita-Sando Station that opened in late 2003. It has 1970s Altec speakers modified by the owner and a mixer set up for David Mancuso's visits to Japan, and is a small venue where underground DJs from Japan and abroad gather and people come for the selections. RA lists events in July 2025 and July 2026, so it is still operating.
