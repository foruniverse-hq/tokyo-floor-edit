What to expect here

A DJ bar opened in 2014 by Global Hearts (formerly Vision/Contact, now operating ENTER), with a Rey Audio sound system. Tokyo-scene veterans take residencies by day of the week, such as DJ EMMA (Thursday HOUSE-TEX) and Nori, and the crowd is mainly music-loving adults. RA lists events through August 2026, and with entry at ¥1,000-1,500 there is no sign of a pickup scene.
