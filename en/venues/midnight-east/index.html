What to expect here

A club-night name under which Spotify O-EAST and the DJ bar Azumaya combine late at night. Bookings are clearly sound-first and RA regulars (DJ NOBU, Floating Points, DJ Harvey, Amelie Lens & Laurent Garnier, Regis), and Regis's January 2026 show is confirmed on the official schedule. Large hip-hop nights such as VERDY's WASTED NIGHT are exceptions; note that it is a night name run in a live house, not a permanent club.
