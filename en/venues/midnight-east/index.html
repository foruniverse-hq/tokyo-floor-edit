About this venue

MIDNIGHT EAST is a club night formed by the merger of Spotify O-EAST and DJ Bar Higashimaya, featuring a clear focus on music with bookings like DJ NOBU, Floating Points, DJ Harvey, Amelie Lens & Laurent Garnier, and Regis—regulars on RA. While occasional hip-hop events like VERDY's WASTED NIGHT exist, it's primarily a live house-based night, not a permanent club, with Regis set to perform in January 2026.
