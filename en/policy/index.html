Editorial policy & privacy

Who decides, what we collect, who runs this.

Editorial independence

Labels, the six criteria and tiers are decided by the editorial side alone. Venues, promoters and advertisers have no say. If we run paid slots they are marked as such and kept apart from ratings. The details are in How we rate.

What we collect

  • Standard web server logs only (IP address, time, page, browser type). We do not collect names or email addresses on this site.
  • No cookies. The only thing stored is your answer to the age check, kept in your browser’s localStorage. It stays in your browser and is never sent to us.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so that request may be logged by Google’s servers.

External links

Event links (Resident Advisor, venue sites) and the subscribe form go to third-party sites. Their own policies apply from there.

Age

This site lists club events for adults 20 and over. 20+ only, photo ID required.
